MY RECOMMENDATION
A — Lucide is the natural choice for this stack. It ships with Next.js docs, used by Vercel, Radix, and shadcn/ui. The thin 2px stroke matches Inter's optical weight perfectly — both were designed with the same geometric precision. Phosphor (B) is more expressive but heavier — better suited for marketing sites than learning tools. Tabler (C) is excellent but slightly rounder, which fights the editorial sharpness of Slate/Parchment.
